# Comparison with public results

Search date: 18 November 2026. The findings below describe public tables and dated source code. They do not establish worldwide priority.

**Projective meanders.** Lando and Zvonkin give the fixed-label definition, the cyclic action, the prime-power congruences and values through order 15. The OEIS entry credits Howroyd for extending the values to 22. The package and Git repository examined in this review contain the OEIS projective record and no separate term-extension file for A006663. [Lando and Zvonkin, §§2–3](https://www.labri.fr/perso/zvonkin/Research/proj-mean.pdf) · [OEIS A006663](https://oeis.org/A006663) · [official table](https://oeis.org/A006663/b006663.txt)

**Rotations and minimum cut height.** Lando and Zvonkin explicitly identify rotation orbits of chord diagrams with unrooted plane trees and exclude a half-turn for a connected projective diagram of order greater than two. The encoding of a rotationally fixed matching without diameters by one balanced binary period also has a published predecessor. For all noncrossing matchings on $2md$ sites invariant under a rotation of order $d\ge3$, the known fixed count is $\binom{2m}{m}$. Alexandersson, Linusson, Potka and Uhlin refine the count by the number of short edges. Bousquet-Mélou and Krattenthaler study rotations of plane trees with prescribed vertex degrees. [Lando and Zvonkin, pp. 235–236](https://www.labri.fr/perso/zvonkin/Research/proj-mean.pdf) · [Alexandersson et al., §4 and Appendix B.1, Bijection 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.11157) · [Bousquet-Mélou and Krattenthaler, §11.1, Corollary 26](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.18656)

Two connected projective diagrams can have different minimum cut heights even when their dual trees have the same degree distribution. On labels 0 through 9, take the chords $(0,9),(1,8),(2,5),(3,4),(6,7)$. Mark successively the faces at the gaps just before labels 0 and 7. Both threefold lifts have six leaves, six vertices of degree two and four of degree three in the dual tree. Direct inspection of their diameter cuts gives minima three and one, respectively.

The comparison also covered annular noncrossing permutations and antipodal chord diagrams for nodal sets. Kim counts annular permutations by cycle types and calls a cycle connected when it meets both boundary circles. Eremenko, Jakobson and Nadirashvili glue a chord diagram to its antipodal copy to realize nodal configurations. Searches in English, French and Russian by subject, together with the public index A006663, located no statement that the fixed counts of connected projective meanders at each minimum height are independent of the odd rotation order for fixed $m$. Results formulated with other statistics or terminology may escape these searches. [Kim, §2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1210.7353) · [Eremenko, Jakobson and Nadirashvili, proof of Theorem 1.1](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~eremenko/dvi/spheresubm1.pdf) · [OEIS A006663](https://oeis.org/A006663)

**Symmetric open shapes.** Legendre's conventions are those of A223096. The official b-file stops at index 22. Hunter Hogan's PyPI release 0.40.1 on 17 November 2026 at 12:22 UTC includes indices 23–31. The Git commit at 14:25 UTC on the same day also contains index 32. All ten values agree with our computations. Relative to that public repository, the extension begins at index 33. The repository also publishes symmetric counters with multiple choices of transfer. [Legendre, Proposition 6.1](https://ajc.maths.uq.edu.au/pdf/58/ajc_v58_p275.pdf) · [published wheel](https://pypi.org/project/mapFolding/0.40.1/) · [pinned table through 32](https://github.com/hunterhogan/mapFolding/blob/1862930c2f0bfe5a38ae8cd299e6b85eaa1dc632/mapFolding/oeis/terms/files/A223/b223096.txt)

**All open shapes.** The published package and Git repository give physical crossing counts through 59 in the separate file for A077055. This corrects a comparison based solely on the official file, which stopped at physical length 43. Additional odd lengths can also be inferred wherever the already public symmetric count and ordinary closed count overlap, in particular through 65. The first odd shape requiring an index beyond the located public symmetric inputs is therefore length 67. The public even-subsequence and labelled-count tables are checked separately. A077054 lists labelled even open counts. **Ordinary semi-meander diagonals.** Zhang and Ma proved the third and fourth closed forms, and for every fixed deficit proved the degree, bounded period and positive constant leading coefficient of the eventual quasipolynomial. Ferudun had already implemented the fixed-deficit FIFO transfer. The exact rational candidates for diagonals five through eleven are published in Hogan's code. We identify these same arrays by release and hash; the release describes their general validity as conjectural. Our contribution is the multiplicity and numerator bound and the exact certificates selecting these seven rational functions on their full domains. [Zhang and Ma](https://oeis.org/A400429/a400429.pdf) · [Ferudun's program](https://github.com/hunterhogan/mapFolding/blob/1862930c2f0bfe5a38ae8cd299e6b85eaa1dc632/research/matrixMeanders/fixed_deficit_semimeanders.py) · [Hogan's exact arrays and status](https://github.com/hunterhogan/mapFolding/blob/1862930c2f0bfe5a38ae8cd299e6b85eaa1dc632/mapFolding/oeis/A400429.py)

**Search coverage.** The review includes the defining papers, their forward citations indexed in OpenAlex (74 and 9 records, respectively), public b-files, repository and Zenodo searches, and searches of Crossref and OpenAlex under projective meanders, symmetric meander shapes, and symmetric foldings. A private search of the OEIS bulk snapshot compared triples from both numerical tables, together with doubled and alternate-index variants, across 401,617 sequence prefixes. Such a scan cannot find values present only in longer b-files, software, differently normalized sequences or poorly indexed sources. The public release of mapFolding is an actual instance in which checking software changes the priority conclusion. The search queries were by subject or public identifier; no unpublished terms were uploaded. [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org/) · [OEIS bulk data](https://oeis.org/stripped.gz)

The closed-meander sequence A005315, open shapes, semi-meanders and projective meanders have separate indexing and public frontiers. Caroppo, Da Lozzo, Di Battista and Vihrovs bound the growth of ordinary meandric numbers and study the consequences for planar graph algorithms. That result concerns a different quantity from the running time of the antipodal counter. [A005315](https://oeis.org/A005315/b005315.txt) · [Caroppo et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11944)
