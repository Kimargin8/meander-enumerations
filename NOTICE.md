# Sources and attribution

The manuscripts and their computational supplements are by Iaroslav Argunov. The following sources contribute independent software, comparison data and earlier mathematical results.

**OEIS reference data.** The numerical tables in `references/oeis/` and the `terms` fields of `data/published.json` come from *The On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences*, published by the OEIS Foundation Inc. with its sequence contributors. The JSON records give the source b-file address and checksum. OEIS reference material is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. The sequence supplement identifies the sources of the earlier values and documents changes to their arrangement, including the reindexing of A005315 values in the first column of A400429. [The On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences](https://oeis.org/) · [OEIS terms](https://oeis.org/wiki/The_OEIS_End-User_License_Agreement) · [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

**Hunter Hogan, *mapFolding*.** Release 0.40.1 and the pinned public repository supply the separately identified `additional_terms` in `data/published.json`. The published candidate polynomials in `experiments/runs/rainbow42/fifo/candidates.json` were extracted from the module `mapFolding/oeis/A400429.py` and transcribed as JSON arrays. The extraction record gives the module and release checksums and the change of origin used for comparison. Hogan publishes this material under CC BY-NC 4.0. The timing study used a separately obtained, unmodified copy of the public C++ source. [Release](https://pypi.org/project/mapFolding/0.40.1/) · [Pinned source tree](https://github.com/hunterhogan/mapFolding/tree/1862930c2f0bfe5a38ae8cd299e6b85eaa1dc632) · [Upstream license](https://github.com/hunterhogan/mapFolding/blob/1862930c2f0bfe5a38ae8cd299e6b85eaa1dc632/LICENSE) · [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)

**Independent solvers.** The independent ordinary open count at 46 crossings was computed locally with Osipov's first-crossing solver. Its calculation record gives the revision, run arguments and binary checksum. The upstream source carries the Apache-2.0 license. The ordinary frontier solver included in `research/meander_lab_v2/cpp` comes from Meander Lab and retains its MIT copyright and permission notice. [Osipov's pinned implementation](https://github.com/zouxuoz/first-crossing-meanders/tree/be0a3f5e5d59d984171a2183dcf30acd1df46885) · [Meander Lab license](research/meander_lab_v2/LICENSE)

The bibliographies in the four manuscripts identify the mathematical predecessors. The comparison with earlier work, including the date of the search, appears in [data/priority.md](data/priority.md).

Earlier two-terminal run records fingerprinted the Python driver present on disk when the record was saved. The numerical solver and terminal reader were fingerprinted when built. Archived driver sources matching the earlier recorded fingerprints are in `protocol_sources/symmetric`. The embedded terminal weights can also be read directly with the supplied Python verifier.
